The normative principles are maintained in
[CANONICAL_CLANK_ARCHITECTURE_v0.1.md](CANONICAL_CLANK_ARCHITECTURE_v0.1.md).

[ADR-0004](adr/0004-secondary-review-rule-integration.md) are mandatory only
