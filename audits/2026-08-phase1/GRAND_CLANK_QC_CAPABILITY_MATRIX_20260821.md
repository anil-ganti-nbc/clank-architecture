## Fleet conclusions

1. **Only Watch Clank implements the target QC vocabulary with durable correction history.** Its Phase 9 addition of DUPLICATE (stop force-filing duplicates as FALSE_POSITIVE) is exactly the kind of vocabulary refinement Grand Clank will need.
2. **OEM Radar has the most tragic QC artifact:** a complete, well-shaped review subsystem that has never received a single review while its benchmark proved 0/50 recall — the human feedback loop that would have caught it was designed but never operated.
3. **Semiconductor Intelligence's mandatory human entity resolution is the strongest *structural* QC** (labeled decisions, append-only trail), but it is entity-centric rather than event-QC and its vocabulary would need an adapter mapping into any fleet standard.
4. **Four Clanks have zero human-feedback capability** (smartwatch, FGT, CTW effectively, tablet partially) — their future training data does not exist and cannot be reconstructed retroactively without run-history tables (which FGT lacks entirely).
5. **QC asymmetry is therefore fleet-wide P1 debt:** human review exists where it was recently audited (watch), was designed-but-abandoned (oem-radar), or was forced by domain necessity (semint) — nowhere else.
